Legal & Policies

Please read the following policies carefully before purchasing any service or product from As Told By Danii.

Last Updated: 2025

Overview

By purchasing any service or product from As Told By Danii ("we," "us," or "our"), you agree to the terms and conditions outlined in this policy. These policies apply to all services, digital products, and physical merchandise offered through As Told By Danii.

Digital Products — No Refund Policy

All sales of digital products are final. No refunds will be issued for any digital product once it has been purchased and/or delivered.

Digital products include, but are not limited to:

  • Digital downloads (templates, guides, workbooks, resources)
  • Online courses or digital course materials
  • Digital brand kits or files
  • Any electronically delivered content

By completing your purchase, you acknowledge that digital products are non-returnable and non-refundable. Please review all product details and descriptions before purchasing. If you have questions about a digital product before buying, contact us at info@astoldbydanii.com.

Custom Apparel & Merchandise Policy

All custom apparel and merchandise orders are made-to-order. All sales are final. No returns or refunds are accepted on custom items.

Minor Updates — 2-Week Window

Clients have a 2-week window from the date of delivery to request minor updates to their custom apparel order. Minor updates may include small adjustments to placement, sizing of a design element, or color correction — at our discretion.

Requests for minor updates must be submitted in writing via email to info@astoldbydanii.com within the 2-week window. After this period, no further updates will be accepted.

Errors Made by As Told By Danii

If an error occurs as a result of a mistake made on our end (i.e., the product was produced differently than what was approved in the mockup), we will replace the item at no additional cost to you. This applies only to errors that are clearly our fault and differ from the approved final design.

Replacements are provided in lieu of refunds. No monetary refunds will be issued under any circumstance — including in cases where an error was made on our behalf.

What Is Not Covered

  • Change of mind after an order has been confirmed and approved
  • Sizing issues where the size was correctly produced as ordered
  • Dissatisfaction with a design that was approved by the client prior to production
  • Damage caused after delivery
  • Orders where incorrect information was provided by the client

Coaching & Service Sessions

All coaching, consulting, and service sessions are scheduled through our booking system. Cancellations and rescheduling policies are handled at the time of booking. We ask for at least 24 hours' notice for any cancellations or rescheduling requests.

Service packages and custom project agreements are governed by the terms outlined in any written agreement or proposal provided at the start of your project.

Questions About These Policies

If you have any questions about our policies, please don't hesitate to reach out before completing your purchase. We're happy to clarify anything.
